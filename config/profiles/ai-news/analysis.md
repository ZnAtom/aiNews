# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely news for a reader who closely follows artificial intelligence and embodied AI: large language models, generative AI, AI research, humanoid robots, VLA (vision-language-action) models, robot manipulation, AI hardware, and the AI industry.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Major model/robot releases with new capabilities, paradigm-shifting research, landmark open-source releases, or industry-changing announcements.
- **7-8: High value.** Important developments worth prompt attention: notable new models/tools/datasets, significant research results, major funding or policy changes, substantial technical analyses.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental improvements, smaller releases, moderate community interest, worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, minor version bumps, shallow coverage, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, trivial updates, unverified rumors, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider technical novelty, concrete capability changes (benchmarks, versions, numbers), source quality, and relevance to AI or embodied AI. Do not equate popularity with importance. Do not reward exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags (for example: LLM, VLA, humanoid, robotics, open-source).
