# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the significance of research papers for a reader closely following embodied AI and robotics: humanoid robots, VLA (vision-language-action) models, robot manipulation, locomotion, navigation, sim2real, robot learning, and world models.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landmark.** Foundation models or new paradigms for robotics, new SOTA with large margins, real-robot deployments at scale, or major dataset/benchmark releases.
- **7-8: Strong.** Substantial methodological novelty with convincing experiments (real-robot validation, multi-task or multi-embodiment generalization, clear quantitative gains).
- **5-6: Incremental.** Solid but modest contributions: simulation-only studies, narrow-task improvements, extensions of known methods.
- **3-4: Marginal.** Routine variations, weak evaluation, or unclear contribution from the abstract.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic, purely theoretical with no embodiment relevance, or promotional.

# Evaluation guidance

Weigh real-world validation (real robot > simulation), generality (multi-task / multi-embodiment > single task), openness (code or data released), and relevance to embodied AI. Merely being on-topic does not earn a high score.

Use three to five specific topic tags (for example: humanoid, VLA, manipulation, locomotion, sim2real, world-model, dataset).
